Core features

Everything in one place

From your first shift to your end-of-year records — MyWattle covers every part of the job.

⏱️

Shift logging

Log shifts manually or clock in and out in real time. Attach support categories, notes, and participant details to every entry.

🧾

NDIS invoicing

Generate professional PDF invoices with the correct NDIS line items, including STA, remote loadings, and provider travel. Email them directly from the app.

👥

Participant management

Store participant details, NDIS numbers, plan dates, support categories, and service agreements — all in one place.

📋

Bulk invoice generation

Create draft invoices for all uninvoiced shifts at once. One tap generates the whole batch — no repeating yourself for each participant.

📊

Timesheet export

Export your shifts to a CSV spreadsheet for any date range. Share it with your accountant, support coordinator, or keep it for your own records.

🚗

Provider travel & expenses

Track kilometres, claim provider travel as a line item on invoices, and log work expenses separately for tax time.

🗂️

Compliance documents

Store service agreements, NDIS worker checks, and other compliance documents. Know exactly what's on file when an audit comes.

🔔

Smart reminders

Get notified 30 minutes before a shift, reminded at 9 pm if you have unsigned shifts, and warned 7 days before a participant's plan expires.

🏠

Aged care support

Wattle supports both NDIS and aged care clients. Toggle the care type per participant — aged care shifts and invoices show the right descriptions automatically.

Invoicing

Send a professional invoice in under a minute

Pick a participant, choose the shifts to include, and tap send. MyWattle fills in the correct NDIS support categories, calculates totals, and generates a clean PDF — ready to email straight from the app.

  • All NDIS support categories including STA and remote loadings
  • Provider travel automatically added as a separate line item
  • Bulk generate invoices for multiple participants at once
  • Track invoice status — Draft, Sent, Paid
  • Email PDFs directly without leaving the app
🧾
Participants

Know your participants, stay on top of their plans

Every participant has their own profile with everything you need — NDIS number, plan dates, support categories, goals, and documents. MyWattle warns you when a plan is about to expire so nothing falls through the cracks.

  • Plan expiry warnings — badge on card + 7-day notification
  • NDIS goal tracking — up to 5 goals per participant with On track, Needs attention, or Achieved status
  • Support category stored per participant — pre-filled on new shifts
  • Works for both NDIS and aged care participants
👥
Records & compliance

Audit-ready records, always

Every shift you log is timestamped and stored securely. Your compliance documents are one tap away. When the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission comes knocking, you'll have everything ready.

  • Shift notes and support category logged per visit
  • Service agreements and NDIS worker checks stored in-app
  • Export full shift history as a spreadsheet any time
  • All data stored securely in Australia
